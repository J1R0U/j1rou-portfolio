THE PHILOSOPHY

“SAFE DESIGN IS THE MOST DANGEROUS THING A BRAND CAN DO.”

Originally from Tago, Surigao del Sur with my creative workplace in Tandag City, I'm Jay'l Gimenez Jr. (known in the design realm as J1rou). I got my start tearing through bold design magazines, underground music posters, and street culture graphics.

Today, I build visual identities, UI/UX interfaces, and graphic collateral that refuse to blend into sterile corporate beige. My work is high-energy, heavy on tactile typography, sharp contrast, and purposeful rebellion.
